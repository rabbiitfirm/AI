## 2025-10-01 - Accessible Modal & Form Field Integration
**Learning:** Converting raw HTML modal overlays to Radix-backed `Dialog` primitives automatically provides focus trapping, ESC key listener, and `sr-only` close buttons while binding form fields with `useId` and `Label`.
**Action:** Use `Dialog` and `useId` along with explicit `isSubmitting` states for form modals to ensure consistent accessibility and submission feedback.
